# Alae

**Your company, your data, your AI.**
Alae connects your systems, structures your company knowledge, and puts it to work with AI running on your own infrastructure.

## Products

| | |
|---|---|
| **[Cortex](https://www.alae-ai.com/produkte/cortex)** – Company Brain | Connects company knowledge and live data streams in one shared context, with source-grounded answers. |

## Why local AI

Customer information, internal decisions, and confidential documents stay inside your company. Alae runs on your own hardware. [Learn more about local AI →](https://www.alae-ai.com/lokale-ki)
